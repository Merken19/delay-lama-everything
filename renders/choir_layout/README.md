# Choir layout: the whole stage, a drum lane and ladders

Scene-preview renders (`:app:delayLamaScenePreview`, every channel but the drums
sung, four monks each) of the choirs' places before and after they moved from
the fan and the right wing's rows to the whole-stage layout (ARCHITECTURE.md,
"The performance's spread"). Vocals are 1.25 times the size in both.

- `compare_spread_0.5.png`, `compare_spread_1.png`: Free Bird (2:00),
  Stairway to Heaven (3:20), Can't Stop (1:00) and Do I Wanna Know? (1:30).
  Columns: before from camera 1A, after from camera 1A, after from high above
  the audience. In Stairway the drums aren't playing at 3:20, so they are not
  drawn.
- `ladder/creep/spread_0.5/scene_60s_far.png`,
  `ladder/do_i_wanna_know/spread_0.5/scene_90s_far.png`: Creep (1:00) and
  Do I Wanna Know? (1:30) from far behind camera 1A, where the heights show:
  each choir standing behind another is 1.1 monks higher.

The other renders made with them (every pose, `layout.txt` with each choir's
place and every instrument's resting box, floor maps) were not kept; re-render
with the scene preview.
