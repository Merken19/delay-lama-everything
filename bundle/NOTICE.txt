4. Bundled extras (soundfonts\, backgrounds\, midi\)
----------------------------------------------------

These are not part of midis2jam2 or Delay Lama; they are included so the
package plays well out of the box.

Timbres of Heaven (XGM) 4.00(G) (soundfonts\) is by Don Allen and others,
licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2:
licenses\Timbres-of-Heaven-GPL-2.0.txt. Its patch list and changelog are next
to it.

The cube map images (backgrounds\tibet\) and the MIDI files (midi\) are
third-party works and belong to their authors. The MIDI files' drum notes
were remapped to General MIDI with Delay Lama Everything's drum fix tool.
